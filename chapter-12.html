Chapter 12

The Fragrance of Green Bamboo Wine, a Gu Master Flaunts His Power

Volume 1 · Demonic Nature Never Changes · Translator: The Omniarch · 2,538 words · ~11 min read
Typeface
← →

“Now, all the problems come down to the Flower-Wine Monk’s treasure. If I can find it, every difficulty will be readily resolved. Should I fail to find it, these difficulties will greatly slow the pace of my cultivation. I would be left far behind my peers at the very outset of my cultivation. How puzzling. I have spent more than a week trying to draw out the Liquor Worm. Why have I still seen no result?”

Fang Yuan’s brow was furrowed as he struggled to find an answer. He did not even notice the taste of the food in his mouth.

Just then, a burst of clamour reached him, interrupting his thoughts.

Fang Yuan looked towards the sound and found six hunters gathered around the table in the centre. They had drunk until they reeked of wine. Their spirits were high, and every face and neck was flushed.

“Younger Brother Zhang, come, have another cup!”

“Elder Brother Feng, we brothers admire your skill. You brought down a black wild boar all by yourself. You are a true hero! You must drink this cup of wine. If you refuse, you will be giving your brothers no face.”

“I thank you, brothers, for thinking so highly of me, but I truly cannot drink any more.”

“Elder Brother Feng cannot drink any more. Is it because he thinks this wine is not good enough? Waiter, get over here! Bring your grandfathers some fine wine!”

Their voices grew louder and louder. Clearly, the hunters had all drunk too much.

The waiter hurried over. “Gentlemen, we do have fine wine, but it is a little expensive.”

“What! Afraid we won’t pay, are you?” On hearing those words, quite a few of the hunters rose to their feet and glared at the waiter. They were either burly and imposing, or swarthy, lean, and sturdy, keen and intimidating, bearing the rugged spirit peculiar to mountain folk.

The waiter hastened to address them, protesting, “How could this humble one presume to look down on you great heroes? It is only that this wine really is a little dear: one jar is worth two primeval stones!”

The hunters were all taken aback.

Two primeval stones was expensive indeed; it was the cost of two months’ living for an ordinary household. Although hunters earned more than ordinary people, at times a single black wild boar was worth half a primeval stone. But hunting was perilous too; at times a hunter might become the prey. Two primeval stones for a single jar of wine was too steep for the hunters; it was not worth it.

“Is there truly wine this costly?”

“Lad, you would not deceive us, would you?”

The hunters shouted and yelled, but their voices were somewhat weak; they were embarrassed and struggling to save face.

The waiter protested again and again that he would never dare.

The hunter known as Brother Feng saw that the scene was going wrong and hastened to smooth things over. “Brothers, let us not spend more. We cannot drink any more today; we shall drink this wine another day.”

“Elder Brother, what a thing to say!”

“This won’t do…”

The rest of the hunters called out still, but their voices had already faded away little by little. One by one, they too sank back into their seats.

The waiter, too, was a shrewd fellow; seeing how matters stood, he understood that the sale would not be made. But this, too, was a scene long familiar to him. He was on the point of withdrawing when, unexpectedly, a young man’s voice rang out from the table in the corner: “Heh, truly amusing. What are you all clamouring about over nothing? If you cannot afford the wine, then hold your tongues properly and cower off to one side!”

When the hunters heard these words, one of them was stung into a sudden roar: “Who says we cannot afford it? Waiter! Bring up that jar of wine! Your father will give you the primeval stones. Aren’t they just two anyway?”

“Yes, honoured guest, one moment! Right away!” The waiter had not expected such a turn of events and answered at once. Turning on his heel, he went downstairs and returned with a jar of wine in his arms.

The jar itself was only half the size of an ordinary wine jar, yet the moment the sealing clay was struck open, a clear, mellow fragrance of wine wafted out and filled the entire dining hall.

The old man drinking alone by the window was also drawn by this fragrance. He could not help turning his head, letting his gaze fall upon the jar.

Fine wine indeed.

“Honoured guests, this humble one is not boasting. This is the finest Green Bamboo Wine; in all of this mountain village, only our inn serves it. Smell it!” Even as the waiter said this, he drew a deep breath, his face wreathed in delight and contentment.

A thought stirred in Fang Yuan’s heart; what the waiter had said was not empty boasting, after all. Gu Yue Village had three wine shops, and what they sold was much the same: ordinary rice wine and cloudy wine, with scarcely any difference between their kinds. In order to draw out the Liquor Worm, Fang Yuan had bought wine for seven consecutive days, so naturally he was well versed in the village’s wine market.

As the hunters gazed at the wine jar before them, a craving for wine stirred in each of them. One by one they twitched their noses, their Adam’s apples rolling.

And the hunter whose tongue had outrun him, the buyer of the wine, wore an even more colourful expression, now with a shade of rueful vexation.

Just this one jar of wine cost two whole primeval stones!

“I bought this wine on a moment’s impulse. And this waiter was far too underhanded, bringing the wine up at once; now the sealing clay is already broken open, so even if I wished to send it back, there is no way to do so.”

The more the hunter thought about it, the more his heart ached. He wished to send it back, yet he truly could not bring himself to lose face.

In the end, he could only slap the table and force a laugh. “Damn it, this wine is fine! Elder brothers, drink to your hearts’ content! Today’s wine is your brother’s treat!”

Right at that moment, the young man at the table in the corner let out another derisive laugh. “With just this one small jar of wine, how is it enough for six men to drink? If you have the guts, buy a few more jars!”

Goaded by these words until his veins bulged and throbbed, the hunter sprang to his feet in a towering rage, his eyes glaring wide at the young man who had spoken. “You little bastard, you have plenty to say for yourself! Come on, step out, and spar a round or two with your elder brother!”

Hearing these words, the young man actually rose to his feet. “Oh? Then I shall step out.” With a sinister smile, he walked out from the shadows in the corner.

He was tall and thin, with a pale complexion, and dressed in dark-blue martial attire that looked neat and trim. On his head he wore a sapphire-blue headband, and on his upper body a short jacket, exposing his thin, frail shoulders. Below, he wore long trousers, bamboo-and-sedge shoes on his feet, and leg wraps at his calves. Most crucial of all, a blue cloth belt was fastened around his waist. Inlaid at the centre of the belt was a gleaming bronze plate, upon which was engraved the black character “One”.

“A First Revolution Gu Master?!” The blustering hunter clearly understood what this attire signified; he drew in a breath of cold air, and the anger ebbed from his face, turning into terror. He had never imagined that he had actually provoked a Gu Master!

“Didn’t you want to spar a round or two with me? Come on, then. Make your move.” The young Gu Master strolled over with a mocking smile on his face.

Yet the hunter who had provoked him a moment ago stood on the spot like a statue, completely motionless.

“Or you can all come at me together; that will do just as well.” The young Gu Master walked slowly up to the hunters’ table, speaking very casually.

Every hunter’s expression changed; some whose faces had been flushed red with drink turned pale in an instant. Cold sweat trickled down their foreheads. Restless and ill at ease, they did not dare let out a single heavy breath.

Reaching out one hand, the young Gu Master lifted the jar of Green Bamboo Wine, held it beneath his nose to take a sniff, and laughed, “It truly is fragrant…”

“If it pleases you, Lord Gu Master, take it over and drink it. Count it as this humble one’s apology for having offended you, my lord.” The hunter who had provoked him and blustered hastily cupped his hands in salute, forcing a smile onto his face as he spoke.

Unexpectedly, the young Gu Master’s expression changed in a flash, and with a sharp crash he dashed the wine jar to the floor.

The Gu Master’s face was as cold as ice, his gaze as sharp as a sword, as he growled in a low voice, “Does someone like you even have the right to apologise to me? You hunters really are rich, aren’t you, even richer than me, actually spending two primeval stones to buy wine to drink?! Do you have any idea that I am fretting over primeval stones right now?! And you actually dare, at a time like this, to flaunt your wealth before me! Are you mortals even worthy?!”

“We would not dare, we would not dare!”

“We have offended you, my lord! We deserve ten thousand deaths!”

“We humble ones truly meant no offence! Here are the primeval stones we have on our persons; we beg you, Lord Gu Master, kindly accept them.”

The hunters all sprang to their feet as if a jolt had gone through them, pulling primeval stones from their bosoms. Yet what wealth could these mortals possibly possess? All they pulled out were broken bits and fragments of primeval stones, the largest of which was no more than a quarter of a whole piece.

Yet the young Gu Master did not take these primeval stones; he merely kept sneering as his hawk-like gaze swept across the entire dining hall.

The hunters swept by his gaze all lowered their heads one by one. The old man watching the commotion from the table by the window also hastily turned his head to avoid the Gu Master’s gaze.

Only Fang Yuan watched quietly, without the slightest misgiving.

The attire this young Gu Master had on could be worn only by an official Gu Master; even Fang Yuan was not yet qualified. Only after Fang Yuan graduated from the academy could he collect it from the clan. The character “One” on the bronze plate of the young Gu Master’s belt indicated his status as a First Revolution Gu Master.

Yet he already looked to be well into his twenties, and from the aura of primeval essence emanating from his body, he should be at First Revolution, upper stage. Having begun cultivating at fifteen, he was still only at First Revolution, upper stage in his twenties, which showed that the young man’s aptitude was only Grade D, a notch worse even than Fang Yuan’s. Very likely, he was only a logistics Gu Master, not even counting as a combat Gu Master. Yet even so, he was still more than enough to deal with six burly hunters.

This was the gap in strength between Gu Masters and mortals.

“With strength, one can stand high above others; this is the nature of this world. No, every world is like this: big fish eat small fish, and small fish eat shrimp. It is only that this world shows it a little more nakedly, that is all.” Fang Yuan mused inwardly.

“All right, Jiang Ya, teaching them a lesson is enough; do not make things hard for these mortals. If word gets out, you may not mind losing face, but I certainly do.” At this moment, another young person sitting in the corner spoke up.

Hearing her speak, everyone only then realised that this young person was a woman.

Deflated by his female companion’s words, the young Gu Master named Jiang Ya stopped sneering and did not so much as glance at the primeval stone fragments the hunters were offering up in tribute. Added together, these primeval stones did not even amount to two whole pieces; naturally, he had no interest in them.

With a flick of his sleeve, he walked back towards his seat, delivering a parting threat as he strode off. “Those of you with the guts, keep drinking, and drink Green Bamboo Wine at that! I’d like to see who still dares drink this wine!”

The hunters all hung their heads low, scolded until they looked like six obedient grandsons.

A rich fragrance of wine filled the entire dining hall; as the hunter who had bought the wine smelled it, his heart ached so badly that the muscles on his face twitched. He had spent two primeval stones on this wine, yet had not even drunk a single mouthful!

Fang Yuan stopped his chopsticks; he had already eaten his fill. Smelling this fragrance of wine, his gaze flickered a few times. Suddenly, he took out two primeval stones, set them on the table, and said calmly, “Waiter, bring me a jar of Green Bamboo Wine.”

The whole room froze in astonishment.

The young Gu Master, Jiang Ya, stopped in his tracks at once; the corner of his mouth twitched, and he sucked in a sharp hiss of breath. He had only just delivered his parting threat, and already Fang Yuan had ordered a jar of this wine. Was this not deliberately undercutting him and slapping him in the face?

He turned around, narrowed his eyes, and shot a chilling gaze at Fang Yuan.

Fang Yuan met his gaze composedly, his face unperturbed, without the slightest fear.

Jiang Ya’s gaze flickered, and the chill about him gradually faded away as he sensed the aura of primeval essence on Fang Yuan’s body. Realising Fang Yuan’s status, he broke into a smile at once, as warm and gentle as a spring breeze. “So you are a junior brother from the academy.”

Realisation dawned on the others, and at once the way they looked at Fang Yuan had already changed. No wonder this youth was not the least bit afraid of the Gu Master; as it turned out, he too was a Gu Master. Although he was still attending the academy, in essence he was already different.

“Lord Gu Master, your wine!” The waiter came trotting eagerly over, a fawning smile across his face.

Fang Yuan gave a nod to the young Gu Master, Jiang Ya; carrying this jar of wine in hand, he walked out of the inn.
